# Account suspension preserves shared community records

Account suspension indefinitely removes authenticated access and hides personal profile content and identity from ordinary member surfaces, while preserving records. Only a platform administrator may restore an account after finding the suspension mistaken; proving human control alone is insufficient because people can also advertise or scam. Reports are requests for review, never automatic suspension triggers.

Suspension releases upcoming RSVP and waitlist spots but does not automatically hide or remove owned groups or huddlz. Those receive separate administrator review, because a community may include legitimate participants who must not lose its shared content merely because its owner is suspended. Necessary historical attribution uses a neutral label. Restoration neither revives credentials nor rebooks released spots.

Reports and historical audit entries expire after the existing two-year retention window. The current suspension status and reason remain until restoration so expiration of history cannot silently restore access. Automatic purging is deferred to a separate retention investigation: deleting all records could damage shared history and remove context needed to correct a mistaken suspension. This decision does not prescribe permanent retention of all profile data or expand administrators' access to private groups.
